# 408FARMERS — 408-TECH-APPOINTMENT-1.0.1

The `/tech/` campaign is now a minimal appointment intake instead of a CoverageFit Snapshot funnel.

## Prospect sequence

1. Technology role
2. Homeowner or renter
3. Home, auto, or home + auto review
4. Reason for looking
5. First name and mobile
6. Secure CoverageFit appointment booking

The route does not request date of birth, full legal name, address, driver information, vehicle information, policy documents, or detailed household data. It does not expose anonymous continuation or Snapshot discovery. Appointment booking remains the primary completion action, while the Dylan trust card retains direct text and call choices for visitors who would rather talk first.

## 1.0.1 trust-card refinement

The card below the intake now says “You’ll speak directly with,” displays Dylan’s California license number and agency, and retains visible Text Dylan and Call Dylan choices.

## Delivery

The name-and-phone checkpoint remains a hard delivery gate. CoverageFit D1 persistence and Formspree notification are attempted before the secure appointment handoff. A confirmed lead remains recoverable if the prospect leaves before booking.

No D1 migration is required.
